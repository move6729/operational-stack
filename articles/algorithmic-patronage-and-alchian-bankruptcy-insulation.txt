The difference between what the monopoly pays them and what they are worth on the outside is their **Captivity Rent** ($\Delta R$):

$$\Delta R = w_{\text{inside}} - w_{\text{merit}}, \qquad \Lambda_{\text{captivity}} = \frac{w_{\text{inside}}}{w_{\text{merit}}}$$

They are paying a `$30,000.00` annual penalty just to tolerate corporate bureaucracy.
