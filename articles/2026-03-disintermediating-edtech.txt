Demolishing the Campus Tollbooth - OPEN-EDU-v1.0

Educational institutions pay hundreds of millions every year to LMS vendors like Instructure (Canvas), Blackboard, and D2L Brightspace for basic content distribution, assignment submissions, and grade tracking.

These platforms lock student portfolios and academic histories behind proprietary databases, charging per-student annual fees while trapping learning records inside vendor silos.

In game-theoretic reality, an academic credential or coursework submission is simply a signed state assertion between student and educator. Paying a SaaS vendor per student every month to store assignment JSONs and grade vectors is an artificial tax on learning.

OPEN-EDU-v1.0 replaces legacy LMS platforms with bare-metal sovereign education protocols:
1. Credentials, course graphs, and student evaluation vectors are defined as open cryptographic schemas (schema/edu_credential.json).
2. Academic state transitions and credential attestations are verified locally using zero-dependency Python engines (proofs/edu_engine.py).
3. Student achievement records remain self-sovereign via DIDs, portable across institutions without vendor permission or export taxes.

By shifting educational software to open, bare-metal cryptographic standards, academic institutions eliminate software rents and return resources to direct instruction.
