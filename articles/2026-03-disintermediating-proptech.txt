Eliminating the Real Estate Tollbooth: OPEN-PROP-v1.0

Property managers and landlords pay billions annually to SaaS platforms like Yardi, RealPage, and AppFolio for basic tenant tracking, lease execution, and rent collection.

Beyond extraction fees, centralized PropTech algorithms have come under regulatory scrutiny for algorithmic price-fixing and artificial supply withholding—turning property management software into a cartel coordinator.

In game-theoretic reality, a lease agreement is simply a bilateral state contract between property owner and tenant. Paying a SaaS middleman per unit every month to store lease JSONs is an unnecessary tax on housing and commercial space.

OPEN-PROP-v1.0 replaces proprietary property management platforms with bare-metal local lease verification:
1. Leases and unit vectors are defined as open cryptographic asset graphs (schema/prop_lease.json).
2. Rent state transitions and lease execution are verified locally using zero-dependency Python engines (proofs/prop_engine.py).
3. Identity and tenant credentials verify via sovereign DIDs without exposing tenant PII to third-party data brokers.

By shifting property management to bare-metal open schemas, software rents evaporate and housing transactions regain direct peer-to-peer efficiency.
