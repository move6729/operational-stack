## 2. Why Centralized Diligence and Legacy Bug Bounties Fail

When a funder hires a private intelligence firm, a legacy newsroom, or a centralized bug-bounty portal over KYC banking rails and communicates via Slack, email, or Signal, they construct a **bidirectional vulnerability graph**:

* **The Funder Can Be Coerced:** Banking metadata and engagement letters link the principal directly to the investigation. A cartel counterparty can subpoena the brokerage, obtain a prior-restraint injunction, or retaliate against the funder's regulatory permits before a single line of proof is published.
* **The Researcher Can Be Interdicted:** Pre-publication communication creates a live target. Cutouts can infiltrate the chatroom, run cyberstalking and pre-texting operations (`18 U.S.C. § 2261A`), or intimidate witnesses (`18 U.S.C. § 1512`) while the researcher is still assembling the mosaic.
* **The Broker Can Catch-and-Kill (Bilateral Holdup & The "Letter-of-Marque" `MITM` Trap):** Institutional allocators and sovereign desks often ask why they can't simply grant an informal *Letter of Marque* or retainer to a private intelligence brokerage to gather incoming leaks on their behalf. The answer is that delegating collection to a human broker hands a profit-maximizing middleman **sovereign-grade incoming OSINT deal flow** while guaranteeing that both sides get **Man-in-the-Middled (`MITMed`)**:
  1. *Demand-Schedule Leakage:* Telling a human broker which entity tuples $K_{\text{Target}}$ you are investigating leaks your exact blind spots and litigation/portfolio thesis before a single document is found.
  2. *Arrow's Information Paradox ($C_{\text{copy}}(D) = 0$):* In sequential human bargaining, a whistleblower who hands over a raw digital file for "verification" surrenders all leverage immediately because copying a bitstring costs zero—allowing the broker to steal the dataset and stiff the source.
  3. *Lemon `MITM` (Chaff Dilution):* Firewalled from the primary source, the funder cannot stop the broker from padding $5\%$ real rumors with $95\%$ AI-generated folkloric chaff.
  4. *The "Catch-and-Kill" Auction Flip:* Worst of all, suppose a funder offers a broker $B_{\text{Commission}} = \$50,000$ to find a ledger that exposes a cartel target to $L_{\text{Target}} = \$20,000,000$ in liability. The moment an insider hands that ledger to the human broker, the broker's rational profit-maximizing move is to walk across the street to the **target**, demand a $R_{\text{Suppress}} = \$2,000,000$ consulting retainer to **bury** the file and hand over the whistleblower's name, and pocket $\mathbb{E}[\Pi_{\text{Broker}}^{\text{Flip}}] = \$1,950,000$ net of expected audit penalties ($p_{\text{detect}} = 0.05$, $S_{\text{penalty}} = \$1\text{M}$)—a **$39\times$ payoff incentive to betray the client**.

To close the loop between Honest Sovereign Capital and the $20\text{ W}$ Human Swarm, we must eliminate the Principal-Agent relationship entirely.

### Traffic-Tiered Prover Hygiene: Offline `zk-Email` vs. Live `MPC-TLS`

A critical operational discipline separates static archival proofs from live web notarization. When proving an existing `.eml` (`RFC 6376` DKIM) or a hashed docket filing, the $20\text{ W}$ node generates $\pi_{\text{ZK}}$ **100% offline** ($0\text{ bytes}$ of live network traffic). $$B + C_{\text{Gas/ZK}} < \Delta V_{\text{Clearance}} = \tau_{\text{Cartel}} \cdot R_{\text{Prod}} \cdot K_{\text{Prod}} + p_{\text{Shock}} \cdot L_{\text{Contagion}}$$

### Theorem 2: The $M$-Holder Kompromat Hoarding Collapse ($50\times$ Capital Leverage)

Historically, intelligence cutouts hoarded secrets because withholding them paid a recurring blackmail annuity $r_{\text{Hoard}}$ per period (monopoly present value $V_{\text{Hoard}}^{\text{Monopoly}} = \frac{r_{\text{Hoard}}}{1 - \delta}$).

A public stigmergic bounty shatters that monopoly by turning every secret into an **$M$-player preemption race**. Suppose $M \ge 2$ entities (insiders holding overlapping `HNDL` slices plus unencumbered $20\text{ W}$ swarm researchers) each have a per-period probability $\lambda$ of reconstructing $D = \Phi_{\text{Parody}}^{-1}(G_{\text{Folklore}})$ at local cost $C_{\text{Solve}}$. Because the **first** solver to submit $\pi_{\text{ZK}}$ sweeps $100\%$ of $B$ and automatically decrypts $D$ for the entire world, every remaining hoarder watches their private blackmail asset drop to **$\$0$** overnight.

Waiting one more period to collect blackmail rent $r_{\text{Hoard}}$ now carries a survival probability of only $(1 - \lambda)^{M-1}$. The effective value of hoarding collapses to:

$$V_{\text{Hoard}}^{\text{Effective}}(M, \lambda) = \frac{r_{\text{Hoard}} \cdot (1 - \lambda)^{M-1}}{1 - \delta (1 - \lambda)^{M-1}}$$

Plug in real numbers: suppose a corrupt broker extracts $r_{\text{Hoard}} = \$25,000/\text{yr}$ ($\delta = 0.90$), making their monopoly blackmail asset worth **$\$250,000$**. Now let $M = 25$ independent $20\text{ W}$ researchers inspect the public docket mosaic with a $\lambda = 0.15$ period solve probability ($C_{\text{Solve}} = \$200$). The survival factor $(0.85)^{24}$ is just **$0.020233$**—crashing the expected value of hoarding from $\$250,000$ down to **$\$515.20$**.

Honest capital does **not** need to pay $\$250,000$ to outbid the blackmailer. A **$B = \$5,000$** public bounty ($B - C_{\text{Solve}} = \$4,800 \gg \$515.20$) triggers an immediate $t=0$ Prisoner's Dilemma stampede to publish, liquidating a $\$250,000$ kompromat asset at **$50\times$ capital leverage**.
