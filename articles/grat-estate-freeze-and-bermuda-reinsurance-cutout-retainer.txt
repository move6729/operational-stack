## II. What a GRAT Actually Does — and Why the Engine Itself Is Legal

Strip the mysticism from the GRAT and what remains is a two-line algebraic identity.

A Grantor Retained Annuity Trust works like this: the transferor moves appreciating assets into an irrevocable trust, keeps a fixed annuity for a term of years, and names the children as remainder beneficiaries. `26 U.S.C. § 2702` and `26 U.S.C. § 7520` govern the valuation: the retained annuity is priced at the IRS actuarial tables using the hurdle rate `r_7520` — statutorily `120%` of the mid-term applicable federal rate.

The taxable gift is simply the residual:

    Gift_Taxable = V - PV_Retained(r_7520, n, A)

Set the annuity to the "zeroed-out" level — the annuity-certain that amortizes exactly the corpus `V` at the hurdle — and the residual collapses to zero:

    A* = V * r_7520 / (1 - (1 + r_7520)^-n)   =>   PV_Retained = V   =>   Gift_Taxable = 0

That is the entire trick, and it is lawful. Every dollar of appreciation above the hurdle migrates to the remainder beneficiaries free of gift and estate tax:

    Appreciation_Excluded = V * [(1 + g)^n - (1 + r_7520)^n]     for asset CAGR g > r_7520

At the locked illustrative parameters — `$1,000.0M` corpus, `3.0%` hurdle, four-year term, `10%` asset growth — the zeroed-out annuity is `$269.0270M` per year and `$338.59M` of appreciation exits the gross estate. The boundary regimes travel with the identity: at `g = r_7520` the excluded appreciation is exactly `0` (the freeze transfers nothing), and at `g = 2.0% < r_7520` it is `-$43.08M` (the annuity over-distributes and the freeze destroys value). This is the engine that every ultra-high-net-worth family in America may run — and it is the engine the primary record actually documents, naming the instrument as the "2006 Grantor Retained Annuity Trust ('2006 GRAT')" and recording that, absent corrective structuring, "the estate tax would have been around $500 million."

**The anomaly audited here is not the engine. It is the `79.0x` price paid to an unlicensed cutout for assembling it.**
