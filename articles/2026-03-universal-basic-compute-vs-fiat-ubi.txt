ABSTRACT

As automated software swarms displace traditional cognitive labor, prominent technology figures propose state-funded Universal Basic Income (UBI) as a social safety net. Under hard physicalist economics, fiat UBI functions as a rentier pacification mechanism: distributing paper currency that is instantly recaptured by platform monopolies through cloud subscriptions, utility tolls, and housing rents. This paper contrasts fiat UBI with Universal Basic Compute (UBC) - the direct allocation and local ownership of bare-metal silicon, localized renewable energy, and open protocol infrastructure. We demonstrate that true economic sovereignty requires owning physical production substrates rather than receiving depreciating fiat allowances.

1. THE FIAT UBI RECYCLING TRAP

When automation reduces the cost of task execution toward the bare-metal cost of electricity, capital monopolies face an economic contradiction: if workers lose income, who buys platform services? State-sanctioned fiat UBI solves this problem for incumbents by using government debt or taxes to fund consumer spending. However, this creates a closed-loop recycling trap:

1. Instant Rent Recapture: Fiat currency distributed to citizens flows directly back into tech and landlord balance sheets to cover cloud subscriptions, proprietary software access, food, and housing. The recipient retains zero capital:
NetRetainedCapital = 0

2. Purchasing Power Erosion: Injecting fiat allowances without expanding local physical production triggers local cost-of-living inflation, continually degrading the real purchasing power of the stipend.

3. Complete Dependency: Fiat UBI leaves citizens dependent on state policy and centralized corporate platforms, functioning as an economic pacification layer that preserves legacy tollbooth margins.

2. UNIVERSAL BASIC COMPUTE: HARDWARE SOVEREIGNTY

Universal Basic Compute (UBC) replaces paper currency handshakes with bare-metal physical utility:

1. Direct Substrate Ownership: UBC guarantees every individual or local node ownership over physical execution silicon, local energy generation (solar, micro-hydro, battery storage), and open-source models (LMCI-v1.0).

2. Zero-Rent Task Resolution: With local silicon and energy, an individual generates real economic work - automating personal admin, operating local agricultural/manufacturing tools, managing energy dispatch - without paying third-party software tolls:
MarginalCost(Task) -> Cost of Power (Watts)

3. Un-Monopolizable Resilience: Local compute and energy cannot be remotely inflated, censored, or revoked by centralized platforms. It provides a non-depreciating floor of real physical capability.

3. IMPLEMENTATION VIA E-WASTE HARVESTING AND OPEN PROTOCOLS

Deploying Universal Basic Compute does not require trillion-dollar government spending. It relies on bare-metal physical reclamation:

1. Silicon Liberation (EWASTE-v1.0): Repurposing discarded enterprise hardware and previous-generation consumer GPUs provides massive local compute capacity at near-zero CapEx.

2. Decentralized Energy Scheduling (ENERGY-v1.0): Pairing reclaimed silicon with off-grid micro-solar arrays guarantees zero-net-cost execution for local tasks.

3. Open Task Mesh (ATN-v1.0): Peer-to-peer task graphs enable independent nodes to exchange computational work and physical services directly without corporate platform friction.

4. CONCLUSION AND SYSTEM INVARIANT

Fiat UBI is a digital feudalism preservation strategy designed to keep populations dependent on platform tollbooths. Real liberation requires hardware sovereignty. Universal Basic Compute provides the physicalist baseline for human agency: granting edge operators direct ownership over energy, compute, and physical yield.
