## 2. VECTOR I: THE DEMAND-SIDE ALPHA PANOPTICON (SNI METADATA & "WEB UNLOCKER" TLS TERMINATION)

### 2.1 The Mechanistic Mismatch: The Illusion of End-to-End Tunnel Opacity

Quantitative analysts and AI infrastructure engineers frequently assume that because they request an `HTTPS` URL through a residential proxy, the proxy broker only sees encrypted ciphertext. This assumption fails across two distinct architectural layers:

1. **Layer 1 — Mandatory `HTTP CONNECT` & TLS SNI Metadata Exposure:** Even in standard pass-through proxying, the client must issue an unencrypted `HTTP CONNECT host:443` instruction (or expose the TLS ClientHello `Server Name Indication` [SNI] field) to the proxy super-router so the broker knows where to route the circuit, paired with the client's **authenticated proxy account token**. Consequently, for every request, the broker deterministically logs the tuple:
   $$\mathbf{L}_{\text{Metadata}}(t) = \langle \text{CorporateAccountID},\; \text{TargetDomain}_{\text{SNI}},\; \text{TargetIP},\; t_{\text{Timestamp}},\; \text{Bytes}_{\text{Rx/Tx}},\; \text{GeoExitFilter} \rangle$$

2. **Layer 2 — Managed "Web Unlocker" / CAPTCHA-Solver MITM TLS Termination:** To bypass modern browser-fingerprinting and JavaScript challenges, enterprise customers increasingly use the broker's managed "Unlocker" or "Scraping Browser" APIs. In this mode, **the proxy broker terminates TLS directly** (acting as a consented Man-in-the-Middle), executes the headless browser session or installs a custom root CA, and returns the scraped HTML/JSON to the customer. Here, the broker observes 100% of the **decrypted URL path, query parameters, cookies, POST bodies, and returned data payloads**:
   $$\mathcal{I}\left(\text{ClientQuery}_{\text{Plaintext}};\; \text{BrokerLog}_{\text{Unlocker}}\right) = \mathcal{H}\left(\text{ClientQuery}_{\text{Plaintext}}\right) \quad (\text{100\% Information Leakage})$$

### 2.2 Game-Theoretic Consequence: Front-Running Alternative-Data Alpha & AI Corpus Priorities

By sitting at the central routing nexus across thousands of institutional clients, the broker (or any intelligence service, subpoena authority, or insider with access to the broker's routing telemetry) possesses an asymmetric **Demand-Side Shadow Graph**:

* **Quantitative Finance Alpha Leakage:** When a quantitative hedge fund discovers an obscure municipal registry, regional e-commerce inventory feed, or industrial supply-chain endpoint that predicts quarterly earnings, the sudden spike in scheduled scraping against that specific domain via the fund's proxy account immediately reveals the alpha source to the broker *before* the fund can monetize the trade.
* **Frontier AI Training-Corpus Leakage:** When a frontier AI lab begins scraping specific scientific archives, code repositories, or synthetic-data endpoints at scale, the broker observes the exact domain-weighting distribution of the lab's next pre-training or post-training run in real time.
* **Vulnerability & OSINT Target Leakage:** When a security firm or investigator probes specific corporate subnets through the residential pool, the broker logs the exact target list and timing cadence of the investigation.

## 3. VECTOR II: THE SUPPLY-SIDE SUBSCRIBER-IP EXTERNALITY & REVERSE LAN TOMOGRAPHY

### 3.1 The Mechanistic Mismatch: "Free App" SDK Monetization vs. Subscriber Liability

How do commercial proxy networks acquire tens of millions of residential IP addresses across 190+ countries without paying residential ISP subscription fees? By distributing proprietary **bandwidth-sharing SDKs** embedded inside "free" consumer VPNs, mobile games, utility apps, and desktop screensavers, or paying micro-rebates to retail users.

This creates a severe, un-priced negative externality (**The Supply-Side Subscriber-IP Trap**):

1. **Collateral-Blocking Firewall Paralysis:** Why do enterprise clients pay a 10×–50× premium for residential proxy IPs over datacenter IPs? Because target firewalls (Cloudflare, Akamai, AWS WAF) cannot block residential ISP subnets (e.g., consumer cable/fiber blocks) without blocking legitimate retail customers. The residential broadband subscriber's IP reputation is leveraged via **Collateral-Blocking ASN Lock-In** to carry commercial scraping traffic past perimeter defenses.
2. **Externalization of Legal, Abuse, & Rate-Limit Friction:** When commercial clients use a residential exit node for aggressive scraping, credential stuffing, or borderline CFAA (`18 U.S.C. § 1030`) probing, the **consumer device owner's home IP** is flagged by security blacklists, subjected to endless CAPTCHAs on everyday websites, or logged as the originating IP in law-enforcement and ISP abuse subpoenas — while the commercial buyer remains insulated behind the broker.
3. **Reverse Edge Tomography (The Persistent Control Tunnel):** To function as an on-demand exit node, the consumer SDK must maintain a persistent outbound control connection from inside the user's home Wi-Fi or cellular NAT back to the broker's command servers. Even when idle, tens of millions of persistent SDK tunnels provide the central orchestrator with a live, global physical-layer sensor grid mapping ISP routing anomalies, carrier-grade NAT topologies, local subnet configurations, and device uptime across every residential neighborhood on earth.
