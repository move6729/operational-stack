#### 2.1 Heavy-Tailed Spectral Condensation vs. the Marchenko-Pastur Noise Bulk

If $\mathbf{W}$ were an untrained random matrix with i.i.d. entries of variance $\nu^2$, the empirical spectral density (`ESD`) $\rho(\lambda)$ would be bounded strictly inside the Marchenko-Pastur (`MP`) noise bulk:

$$\lambda_{\pm} = \nu^2 \left(1 \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{Q}}\right)^2$$

As Martin and Mahoney (`arXiv:1810.01075`) proved across hundreds of production neural architectures, training on compressed human intelligence drives a phase transition out of the Marchenko-Pastur bulk. High-frequency syntactic filler and statistical noise remain trapped below the bulk edge $\sigma_{\text{MP}} = \sqrt{m \lambda_+}$, while multi-hop logical deduction, cross-domain synthesis, and rare-token algebraic precision condense into a heavy-tailed power-law distribution $\rho(\lambda) \sim \lambda^{-\alpha}$ (typically with tail exponent $\alpha \in [2.0, 4.0]$) above the bulk edge.

$$\text{SNR}_b(\sigma_{\text{tail}}) = \frac{\sigma_{\text{tail}}}{\epsilon_b}$$
