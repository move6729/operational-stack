### 1. The Most Surveilled People on Earth: Why Billionaires Cannot Hide a Secret on Their Phones, Their Jets, or Their Own Public Clouds

The bunker is there to give his servers the one thing he can never buy on his phone, his jet, or his public cloud: a place where the law cannot read the logs. And that is precisely why a free society cannot allow private underground data sanctuaries to exist.
