1. **The "Gates-Up-or-Down" Boundary (*Van Buren v. United States*, 593 U.S. 374 (2021)):** Writing for the Supreme Court, Justice Barrett held at **593 U.S. at 378, 389–390**:
   > *"An individual 'exceeds authorized access' when he accesses a computer with authorization but then obtains information located in particular areas of the computer—such as files, folders, or databases—that are off limits to him... In sum, an individual 'exceeds authorized access' when he accesses a computer with authorization but then obtains information located in particular areas of the computer that are off limits to him under a 'gates-up-or-down' inquiry—one either can or cannot access a computer system, and one either can or cannot access certain areas within the system."*
