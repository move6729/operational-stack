#### 1. `42 U.S.C. § 1981` Contract Parity and *Comcast* (`589 U.S. 327`) Deterministic `But-For` Causation

Enacted in the Civil Rights Act of 1866, **`42 U.S.C. § 1981(a)`** guarantees that all persons within the jurisdiction of the United States shall have the same right *"to make and enforce contracts... as is enjoyed by white citizens,"* defining *"make and enforce contracts"* in `§ 1981(b)` to include *"the making, performance, modification, and termination of contracts, and the enjoyment of all benefits, privileges, terms, and conditions of the contractual relationship."*

In ***Comcast Corp. v. National Assn. of African American-Owned Media***, `589 U.S. 327, 333–41` (2020), the U.S. Supreme Court held that a `§ 1981` plaintiff must plead and prove that race was a **`but-for` cause** of the contractual injury. In traditional human-mediated commerce, proving `but-for` causation is difficult because a human counter-party can cite dozens of subjective, non-racial reasons for offering a different price.

In an algorithmic checkout pipeline, **the SQL code *is* the counterfactual proof**. Consider the exact architecture of a conditional checkout pricing rule:

```sql
CASE
    WHEN merchant_or_user.demographic_flag = TRUE THEN 0.00
    ELSE 3.99
END AS delivery_fee_usd
```

In this code block, holding every single transactional variable constant—the cart items, the delivery distance, the time of day, the courier availability—flipping the boolean variable `demographic_flag` from `FALSE` to `TRUE` deterministically changes the contractual price term from `$3.99` to `$0.00`. The code itself establishes 100% mathematical `but-for` causation under *Comcast*.

#### 2. The Death of the "Benevolent Marketing" Defense: *Fearless Fund* (`11th Cir. No. 23-13138`) and *Roberts v. Progressive* (`6th Cir. No. 24-3454`)

Can a corporation defend a race-gated contractual benefit or fee waiver under `42 U.S.C. § 1981` by characterizing it as "benevolent" corporate philanthropy or expressive marketing speech?

On June 3, 2024, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit answered with an emphatic **no** in ***American Alliance for Equal Rights v. Fearless Fund Management, LLC***, No. `23-13138`, `103 F.4th 765` (11th Cir. June 3, 2024; reversing N.D. Ga. Case No. `1:23-cv-03424-TWT`). Ordering a preliminary injunction against a contest that awarded `$20,000` contractual grants exclusively to businesses majority-owned by Black women, the Eleventh Circuit held that:
* A promotional contest or grant entry with binding rules and consideration is a **contract** governed by `42 U.S.C. § 1981`;
* The First Amendment does not confer a right to exclude persons by race in the formation of commercial contracts (*Runyon v. McCrary*, `427 U.S. 160, 176` (1976)); and
* Ad-hoc corporate marketing programs cannot invoke the narrow remedial affirmative-action exception of *Johnson v. Transportation Agency*, `480 U.S. 616` (1987), or **`29 C.F.R. Part 1608`** when they operate as absolute categorical exclusions rather than temporary, formally documented remedial plans curing a specific, measured manifest imbalance in a traditionally segregated job category.

That ruling was reinforced on February 24, 2026 by the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit in ***Roberts v. Progressive Preferred Insurance Co.***, Case No. `24-3454` (6th Cir. Feb. 24, 2026; affirming and examining N.D. Ohio Case No. `1:23-cv-01597`), which adjudicated a `42 U.S.C. § 1981` challenge to Progressive Corp.'s (`NYSE: PGR`, `CIK 0000080661`) commercial grant program administered alongside Hello Alice (`Circular Board Inc.`). Together, *Fearless Fund* and *Roberts v. Progressive* establish that any commercial contract, fee waiver, credit, or vendor grant that conditions financial terms on protected status outside a strict `29 C.F.R. Part 1608` predicate is *per se* exposed under `42 U.S.C. § 1981`.

#### 3. California's Strict-Liability Multiplier: `Cal. Civ. Code §§ 51, 51.5 & 52(a)` and *Koire v. Metro Car Wash* (`40 Cal. 3d 24`)

If federal `§ 1981` supplies the nationwide `but-for` liability hook, California's Unruh Civil Rights Act (`Cal. Civ. Code §§ 51, 51.5, 52(a)`) supplies the balance-sheet liquidation multiplier. Under `Cal. Civ. Code § 51(b)`, all persons within California are entitled to the full and equal accommodations, advantages, facilities, privileges, or services in all business establishments of every kind whatsoever, regardless of sex, race, color, religion, ancestry, national origin, disability, medical condition, genetic information, marital status, sexual orientation, citizenship, primary language, or immigration status. `Cal. Civ. Code § 51.5(a)` explicitly extends this prohibition to any business establishment that *"discriminate[s] against, boycott[s] or blacklist[s], or refuse[s] to buy from, contract with, sell to, or trade with any person"* on those characteristics.

Crucially, in ***Koire v. Metro Car Wash***, `40 Cal. 3d 24, 29–39` (1985), the California Supreme Court held that **sex- or group-based price discounts** ("Ladies' Day" car wash discounts and "Ladies' Night" bar admission waivers) violate the Unruh Act as a matter of law. The California Supreme Court rejected every single argument that modern growth-marketing teams use today:
* **Profit or Promotional Motive Is No Defense:** Chief Justice Bird wrote for the Court (`40 Cal. 3d at 32`) that while price discounts tied to neutral economic facts (such as children's meals or off-peak hours open to everyone) are permissible, conditioning a price discount on a protected classification is arbitrary and unlawful even if designed to increase revenue or promote social goodwill.
* **No Out-of-Pocket Injury Beyond the Differential Required:** Under `Cal. Civ. Code § 52(a)`, whoever denies, aids, or incites a denial of rights under `§ 51` or `§ 51.5` is liable for each and every offense for the actual damages, and any amount that may be determined by a jury or court up to three times actual damages, ***"but in no case less than four thousand dollars ($4,000), and any attorney's fees that may be determined by the court."*** (*Koire*, `40 Cal. 3d at 33–34`).
