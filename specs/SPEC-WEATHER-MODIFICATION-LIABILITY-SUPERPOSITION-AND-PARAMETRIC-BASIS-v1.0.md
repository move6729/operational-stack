## 1. Executive Summary

Federal weather-modification law is a **notice-only regime**. The Weather Modification Reporting Act of 1972 (`Pub. L. 92-205`, codified at `15 U.S.C. §§ 330–330e`) requires an operator who intends to modify the weather to *file a report with the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration* (`15 C.F.R. Part 908`) — and nothing more. The statute confers no permit, imposes no efficacy standard, and authorizes no downstream-damage compensation. Its sole sanction is a statutory criminal fine capped at `$10,000` for non-reporting (`15 U.S.C. § 330d`: "upon conviction thereof, be fined not more than $10,000"). The regime is a transparency ledger, not a liability gate. As the Government Accountability Office concluded in *Weather Modification: NOAA Should Strengthen Oversight of Reporting* (`GAO-26-108013`, Feb. 2026), NOAA lacks the instrumentation and enforcement posture to reconcile filed reports against observed atmospheric outcomes.

Beneath this notice-only federal floor sits a **common-law proximate-causation split** that is far more consequential than the statute. In `Slutsky v. City of New York`, 197 Misc. 730, 97 N.Y.S.2d 238 (Sup. Ct. N.Y. County 1950), the court held that a plaintiff has **no vested property right in the clouds** and cannot recover for rainfall allegedly diverted by municipal seeding, because the plaintiff cannot prove that the specific precipitation would have fallen on their land but for the intervention. In `Southwest Weather Research, Inc. v. Rounsaville`, 320 S.W.2d 211 (Tex. Civ. App.—El Paso 1958), a Texas appellate court reached the opposite posture, enjoining a commercial seeder whose hail-suppression flights were alleged to trespass on a rancher's atmospheric column and deprive him of rain. The two decisions define a **jurisdictional arbitrage surface**: the same physical act of silver-iodide seeding is either a non-cognizable interference with a public resource (`Slutsky`) or an actionable trespass (`Rounsaville`), depending on the forum.

This specification formalizes the resulting **Weather Modification Efficacy–Culpability Superposition**. A single operator occupies two contradictory legal states *simultaneously and profitably*:

## 2. Empirical, Statutory & Judicial Baseline

### 2.1 The Notice-Only Federal Regime: `15 U.S.C. §§ 330–330e` & `15 C.F.R. Part 908`

The Weather Modification Reporting Act of 1972 (`Pub. L. 92-205`) establishes a **reporting obligation, not a licensing regime**. Under `15 U.S.C. § 330a`, any person who engages in, or attempts to engage in, weather modification within the United States must report the activity to the Secretary of Commerce (delegated to NOAA). The implementing regulations at `15 C.F.R. Part 908` prescribe the form and timing of the report but impose **no efficacy threshold, no environmental-impact precondition, and no downstream-compensation fund**. The National Weather Modification Policy Act of 1976 (`Pub. L. 94-490`) directed a federal research and coordination program but likewise created no private right of action and no strict-liability standard for seeding operators.

The **only sanction** in the statutory scheme is `15 U.S.C. § 330d`, which authorizes a statutory criminal fine **not to exceed `$10,000`** for a knowing failure to report ("upon conviction thereof, be fined not more than $10,000"). This cap is the structural keystone of the arbitrage: the maximum federal downside for operating an unreported seeding program (`$10,000`) is three orders of magnitude below the commercial inflow the program generates (`$6,900,000`) and four orders of magnitude below the externalized downstream damage it can produce (`$35,000,000`). The federal regime is therefore **deterrence-null**: it prices non-reporting as a rounding error.

`GAO-26-108013` (Feb. 2026) confirms the enforcement vacuum. The GAO found that NOAA's reporting intake is not reconciled against independent atmospheric instrumentation, that filed reports are not systematically audited for outcome fidelity, and that the agency lacks the telemetry to distinguish a reported seeding event's marginal contribution from natural variability. The notice-only regime thus produces a **ledger without a balance sheet**: it records intent but never verifies effect, which is precisely the evidentiary gap the operator exploits.

### 2.2 The Common-Law Atmospheric Property & Proximate-Causation Split

The operative liability surface is not federal but common-law, and it is **bifurcated**:

- **`Slutsky v. City of New York`, 197 Misc. 730, 97 N.Y.S.2d 238 (Sup. Ct. N.Y. County 1950).** A landowner sued the City for cloud-seeding that allegedly diverted rainfall. The court dismissed, reasoning that **no vested property right exists in the clouds or in unprecipitated atmospheric moisture**, and that the plaintiff could not establish but-for causation — that the specific moisture would have fallen as rain on the plaintiff's parcel absent the seeding. `Slutsky` is the **causal-impotence pole**: it makes atmospheric interference non-cognizable because the counterfactual (natural rainfall distribution) is unobservable at the parcel scale.

- **`Southwest Weather Research, Inc. v. Rounsaville`, 320 S.W.2d 211 (Tex. Civ. App.—El Paso 1958).** A rancher sought to enjoin a commercial hail-suppression operator, alleging that seeding flights trespassed on his atmospheric column and deprived him of beneficial rain. The Texas court treated the atmosphere above the land as within the landowner's protectable interest and entertained the trespass theory. `Rounsaville` is the **atmospheric-trespass pole**: it recognizes a cognizable property interest in the superjacent airspace and its moisture.

The split is **not reconciled by any federal statute**, and `15 U.S.C. §§ 330–330e` does not preempt state tort law. An operator can therefore select the pole that minimizes expected liability: plead `Slutsky` causal impotence in a `Slutsky`-friendly forum, or settle cheaply to avoid a `Rounsaville`-style injunction. The **jurisdictional selection option** is itself an asset of the superposition.

Internationally, the `ENMOD` Convention (May 18, 1977, `31 U.S.T. 333`) prohibits *hostile* environmental modification but expressly preserves an **Article III `'peaceful purposes'` carve-out**. Commercial and municipal seeding falls squarely inside the carve-out, so `ENMOD` imposes no constraint on the domestic commercial operator and reinforces the notice-only posture.

### 2.3 Parametric Weather Derivatives: CME `HDD/CDD` & Precipitation Indices

The commercial efficacy leg is monetized not only through the seeding contract but through the **parametric derivatives market**. CME Group lists weather futures and options settled against heating degree-day (`HDD`) and cooling degree-day (`CDD`) indices at a multiplier of **`$20` per index point**, alongside parametric precipitation and temperature indices. A parametric contract pays on an **objective index realization**, not on proven loss — the payout triggers when the measured index crosses a strike, irrespective of causation.

This is the **basis-hedge engine** of the superposition. The operator's `$4,500,000` parametric payout corresponds to a notional of `225,000` index points at the `$20` multiplier. Because the derivative settles on the *index* and not on *attributed causation*, the operator can hold a position that pays when weather moves adversely to a hedged exposure **without ever conceding that its own seeding caused the movement**. The parametric instrument is causation-agnostic by construction: it converts an unprovable atmospheric counterfactual into a settled cash flow. This is the derivatives analogue of the `Slutsky` causation bar — both monetize or neutralize the same unobservable counterfactual.

The moral-hazard surface is symmetric: a parametric payout that rewards an adverse index realization can, in the limit, incentivize the very seeding that produces the adverse realization downstream. The notice-only regime provides no telemetry to detect this coupling, and `GAO-26-108013` documents the absence of outcome reconciliation.

### 2.4 Attribution Science & the `GAO-26-108013` Oversight Gap

The stability of the superposition depends entirely on the **attribution gap**: the inability of a plaintiff to prove that a specific downstream flood or drought was caused by a specific upstream seeding event. That gap is quantified by the signal-to-noise ratio of the claimed effect against natural interannual variance. A claimed `+12.0%` precipitation shift inside a basin whose natural interannual standard deviation is `24.0%` yields `SNR_1 = 0.50` — the signal is half the noise. Single-season attribution is statistically impossible; a plaintiff needs many consecutive instrumented seasons to isolate the anthropogenic component.

Two technologies are closing this gap. **Isotopic tracer telemetry** (e.g., silver-iodide and stable-isotope signatures in precipitation and snowpack cores) can fingerprint seeded versus natural moisture at the parcel scale. **Ensemble attribution models** (counterfactual climate ensembles with and without the seeding forcing) can estimate the marginal contribution of an intervention with quantified uncertainty. As these instruments mature, the effective `SNR` rises, `N_min` falls, and proximate-cause probability migrates toward the preponderance bar. `GAO-26-108013`'s finding that NOAA does not reconcile reports against instrumentation is the **regulatory expression of the same gap**: the agency that could close the attribution loop has not been resourced to do so.

## 3. Game-Theoretic & Economic Formalization

### 3.1 EQ_01: Statistical Masking inside Natural Variance

A cloud-seeding operator claiming a precipitation shift $\Delta_\mu = +12.0\%$ inside a basin with natural interannual standard deviation $\sigma_{\text{nat}} = 24.0\%$ generates a single-season signal-to-noise ratio

$$\text{SNR}_1 = \frac{\Delta_\mu}{\sigma_{\text{nat}}} = \frac{12.0}{24.0} = 0.50.$$

Because $\text{SNR}_1 < 1$, the claimed anthropogenic signal is **submerged inside natural variance**: a single instrumented season cannot distinguish the seeding effect from ordinary climatic fluctuation. Under a two-sided $z = 1.96$ ($95\%$ confidence) evidentiary threshold, the number of consecutive independent instrumented seasons required to isolate the anthropogenic mean shift is

$$N_{\min} = \left(\frac{z}{\text{SNR}_1}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1.96}{0.50}\right)^2 = (3.92)^2 = 15.3664.$$

A downstream tort plaintiff must therefore assemble **more than fifteen consecutive instrumented years** of basin telemetry — with the seeding forcing held identifiable across the entire window — before a court can find anthropogenic causation at the $95\%$ confidence bar. No municipal seeding contract, and no `GAO-26-108013`-era NOAA ledger, persists that long with continuous attribution-grade instrumentation. **EQ_01 is the statistical engine of the `Slutsky` causation bar**: the doctrine's "no proof the rain would have fallen here" holding is the legal restatement of $\text{SNR}_1 = 0.50$.

### 3.2 EQ_02: The Efficacy–Culpability Superposition Spread

The operator simultaneously occupies the efficacy pole (sold to the buyer) and the impotence pole (asserted against the claimant). The commercial inflow is

$$P_{\text{contract}} + V_{\text{param}} = \$2{,}400{,}000 + \$4{,}500{,}000 = \$6{,}900{,}000,$$

where the parametric leg settles on $225{,}000$ `HDD/CDD` index points at the CME multiplier $m_{\text{CME}} = \$20$ per point ($225{,}000 \times \$20 = \$4{,}500{,}000$). Against this inflow, the operator invokes `Slutsky` and $\text{SNR}_1 = 0.50$ to suppress the tort recovery probability to $p_{\text{tort}} = 0.04$, yielding an expected liability

$$E[L_{\text{tort}}] = p_{\text{tort}} \cdot D_{\text{ext}} = 0.04 \times \$35{,}000{,}000 = \$1{,}400{,}000.$$

The net superposition spread is

$$S_{\text{super}} = (P_{\text{contract}} + V_{\text{param}}) - E[L_{\text{tort}}] = \$6{,}900{,}000 - \$1{,}400{,}000 = +\$5{,}500{,}000.$$

The spread is **strictly positive** and is captured under a federal regime whose maximum sanction ($F_{\text{stat}} = \$10{,}000$ under `15 U.S.C. § 330d`) is de minimis against both the spread and the expected liability. The operator is paid for efficacy it warrants to the buyer and is simultaneously insulated from the causation it denies to the claimant — the **superposition is the profit**. The parametric derivative is the mechanism that launders the unprovable counterfactual into settled cash: it pays on the index, never on attributed causation.

### 3.3 EQ_03: 2026 Attribution-Science Phase Transition & Superposition Collapse

The superposition is metastable. Its half-life is the time until attribution science closes the $\text{SNR}_1 = 0.50$ gap. When isotopic tracer telemetry and ensemble attribution models push the proximate-cause probability across the preponderance bar $p_{\text{tort}}^{*} = 0.51$, expected liability undergoes a **discontinuous phase transition**:

$$E[L_{\text{tort}}]' = p_{\text{tort}}^{*} \cdot D_{\text{ext}} = 0.51 \times \$35{,}000{,}000 = \$17{,}850{,}000.$$

The operator's net payoff inverts:

$$\Pi_{\text{post}} = (P_{\text{contract}} + V_{\text{param}}) - E[L_{\text{tort}}]' = \$6{,}900{,}000 - \$17{,}850{,}000 = -\$10{,}950{,}000 < 0.$$

The transition is a **sign flip**, not a gradient: the same physical operation that yielded $+\$5{,}500{,}000$ under $\text{SNR}_1 = 0.50$ yields $-\$10{,}950{,}000$ once $p_{\text{tort}}$ crosses $0.51$. At that point the `15 U.S.C. § 330` notice-only shield collapses, because the shield's protective power was never statutory — it was **evidentiary**, resting entirely on the plaintiff's inability to prove causation. Once attribution science supplies the proof, the notice-only regime offers no substantive defense, the `Slutsky` pole is foreclosed, and the operator is driven to the `Rounsaville` pole (actionable trespass) with full downstream damages internalized. The arbitrage is a **wasting asset** whose decay rate is the resolution curve of ensemble attribution.

## 5. Constructive Resolution: Statutory & Engineering Mandates

The superposition is not eliminated by banning seeding — `ENMOD` Article III already preserves `'peaceful purposes'` modification, and municipal water-security seeding has legitimate public value. It is eliminated by **collapsing the evidentiary gap on which the arbitrage rests** and by **internalizing the downstream externality at the commercial boundary**. Three structural mandates suffice.

### 5.1 Mandate 1: Attribution-Grade Instrumentation as a Condition of Reporting

Amend `15 C.F.R. Part 908` so that a filed report is **valid only if paired with attribution-grade telemetry**: continuous basin instrumentation, isotopic tracer signatures, and a counterfactual ensemble baseline deposited with NOAA at the time of the seeding event. This converts the `GAO-26-108013` ledger-without-a-balance-sheet into an auditable record and raises the effective $\text{SNR}$ by making the seeding forcing identifiable across seasons. The operator that cannot instrument cannot claim efficacy; the operator that instruments cannot plead `Slutsky` impotence. The superposition requires *both* claims at once; Mandate 1 makes them mutually exclusive.

### 5.2 Mandate 2: Downstream Externality Internalization at the Commercial Boundary

Attach a **strict downstream-damage internalization** to the commercial efficacy leg: whenever an operator warrants a precipitation shift for consideration ($P_{\text{contract}} > 0$) and holds a parametric position on the same basin index, the operator bears expected downstream damage $E[L_{\text{tort}}] = p_{\text{tort}} \cdot D_{\text{ext}}$ at the *attributed* probability, not the suppressed one. This removes the jurisdictional-selection option (pleading `Slutsky` in one forum, settling in another) by fixing liability to the instrumented attribution rather than to forum choice. The `$10,000` `§ 330d` cap is replaced, for the commercial leg, by a damages floor scaled to $D_{\text{ext}}$.

### 5.3 Mandate 3: Parametric Causation-Coupling Disclosure

Require disclosure of any parametric weather-derivative position whose index is correlated with a basin in which the holder conducts seeding, so that the moral-hazard coupling (a payout that rewards the adverse realization the holder may itself produce) is visible to the regulator and the counterparty. This does not restrict the derivative — it severs the **causation-agnostic settlement** from the seeding act by making the coupling a disclosed, priced exposure. Once disclosed, the parametric leg can no longer silently launder the unprovable counterfactual, and the $V_{\text{param}} = \$4{,}500{,}000$ inflow is netted against attributed downstream damage rather than captured risk-free.

Under all three mandates, the post-attribution regime of EQ_03 becomes the *default* rather than the *terminal* state: the operator internalizes $E[L_{\text{tort}}]'$ continuously, the spread $S_{\text{super}}$ is competed away, and seeding survives only where its efficacy is real enough to survive attribution-grade proof.
