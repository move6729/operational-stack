## 3. Hard Game Theory: Risk Asymmetry Breakdown & *Hostis Humani Generis* Dynamics

When a capital class attempts to enclose the biological substrate while extracting permanent economic rents from the un-enhanced population, the game-theoretic equilibrium collapses into non-cooperative, zero-sum dynamics.

### 3.1 Breakdown of Symmetric Risk

In standard market game theory, actors engage in contract law and peaceful exchange because no actor can completely escape physical risk, physical vulnerability, or environmental collapse. Proprietary biological enclosure breaks this symmetric risk boundary:

$$\text{Risk}(\text{Enclosing Class}) \neq \text{Risk}(\text{Enclosed Population})$$

When the enclosing class operates under asymmetric biological insulation, their incentive to maintain public health infrastructure, environmental stability, or shared physical resources decays to zero.

### 3.2 *Hostis Humani Generis* Structural Mechanics

In maritime and international law, *hostis humani generis* ("enemy of all mankind") defines actors who enclose universal commons (such as international waters) for private toll extraction, making peaceful co-existence impossible within shared legal frameworks.

Structurally, an entity attempting to monopolize the biological substrate triggers an identical game-theoretic response:

1. **Symmetric Risk Collapses:** Eliminates the foundation of shared legal recourse and reciprocal market exchange.
2. **Substrate Enclosure:** Forces non-signatories into permanent biological dependency and rent extraction.
3. **Existential Threat Perception:** Incentivizes the surrounding population to treat the enclosing entity as an un-cooperative structural threat to species continuity.
4. **Physical Air-Gap & Enclosure Paradox:** Destroying shared social equilibrium forces the enclosing class into physical micro-enclosures. Absolute physical air-gapping is an operational impossibility: closed-loop life support, power generation, and environmental systems depend continuously on external industrial supply chains, specialized chemical reagents, and hardware component manufacturing. Severing network egress creates total strategic blindness to macro-environmental shifts, whereas maintaining communications channels breaks the security air-gap perimeter.
5. **Principal-Agent Maintenance Breakdown:** Attempting to eliminate human operational risks by deploying automated kinetic hardware or software-enforced physical security fails at the maintenance and component layer. Micro-enclosures cannot host internal semiconductor fabs, machining facilities, or materials processing. Physical actuators experience continuous mechanical wear, sensor calibration drift, and electronic degradation. This shifts the principal-agent hazard directly to third-party maintenance contractors, software update channels, and hardware technicians—ensuring that physical authority remains bound to whichever operational entities manage component replacement and physical infrastructure.
