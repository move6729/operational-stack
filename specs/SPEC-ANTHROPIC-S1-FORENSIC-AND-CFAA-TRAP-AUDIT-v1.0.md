### 2.4 The Statutory Trap

1. **Section 5(b)(1) (15 U.S.C. § 77e(b)(1)).** It is unlawful to use interstate commerce to transmit any prospectus relating to a security for which a registration statement has been filed unless it meets Section 10. An investor-day presentation of current-quarter revenue and run-rate for the issuer's equity, delivered day 135 of an active registration process, is prospectus-grade conditioning material transmitted outside Section 10 compliance.
2. **Section 5(c) (15 U.S.C. § 77e(c)).** Offers to sell or solicit offers to buy while a registration statement is pending — including market-conditioning communications — fall within § 5(c)'s prohibition; the gun-jumping doctrine captures precisely this pre-effective promotional saturation.
3. **Rule 163B unavailability (17 C.F.R. § 230.163B).** The test-the-waters safe harbor is limited to emerging growth companies. With FY2025 audited revenue of $4,585M, Anthropic exceeds the EGC gross-revenue ceiling by ≈3.7x; the safe harbor is categorically unavailable, and no WKSI analog applies to a first-time registrant.
4. **Rule 135 boundary (17 C.F.R. § 230.135).** The June 1 notice was permissible only as a limited announcement; it did not license 135 days of subsequent quantitative conditioning.
5. **Reg FD (17 C.F.R. § 244.100).** Selective dissemination attaches as a continuing discipline once reporting status is assumed; the pre-filing selective leak is the same conduct Section 5 polices at the registration stage.
6. ***Omnicare* omitted-facts theory (575 U.S. 175, 185–86 (2015)).** A registration statement that prints `$          ` for a quarter whose revenue the issuer simultaneously leaked omits material facts necessary to make the filed statements not misleading; the blank-plus-leak combination is a structural half-truth.
7. ***In re Facebook*, 986 F. Supp. 2d at 487.** Section 11 liability attaches where the registration statement fails to disclose known adverse trends later revealed post-offering; here the trend (Q3 compute-efficiency collapse, § 3.3) was known by day 135 and undisclosed at day 147.
