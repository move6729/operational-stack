### II. FOUR-VECTOR STRUCTURAL REFUTATION

#### 1. Moral Imperative & Multi-Planetary Backup Trope
- **Consensus Narrative:** Humanity requires an off-planet backup colony to insulate species survival against single-point-of-failure terrestrial extinction events.
- **Physical Reality:** An extra-planetary habitat in an ambient vacuum or thin atmosphere ($P \approx 0\text{ kPa}$) possesses zero ambient life-support yield, operating as an ultra-brittle thin-client with infinite baseline OpEx overhead:
  $$\text{OpEx}_{\text{OffPlanetHabitation}} \to \infty$$
- **Requisite Variety Deficit:** Deploying a low-variety, artificial life-support control system ($\mathcal{V}_{\text{Habitat}}$) into a high-entropy cosmic radiation vacuum violates Ashby's Law ($\mathcal{V}_{\text{Control}} \ll \mathcal{V}_{\text{Environment}}$), guaranteeing catastrophic state failure under minor systemic perturbations. Systemic survival scales via high local terrestrial variety ($\mathcal{V}_{\text{Local}} \ge \mathcal{V}_{\text{Env}}$), not by duplicating fragile, energy-starved off-planet nodes.

#### 2. Historical Frontier Analogy
- **Consensus Narrative:** Space colonization is the natural, evolutionary continuation of historical human expansion across terrestrial frontiers (seafaring, western migration).
- **Physical Category Error:** Terrestrial historical frontiers contained free ambient oxygen, liquid water, biological food webs, solar thermal mass, and 1 atm atmospheric pressure. Terrestrial migration shifted populations across *geography* within a shared biological ecosystem. Extra-planetary colonization requires engineering a synthetic biosphere inside a lethal vacuum ($T \approx 2.7\text{ K} - 390\text{ K}$, ionizing radiation, zero magnetosphere):
  $$\text{Baseline Utility}_{\text{Terrestrial Frontier}} > 0 \quad \text{vs.} \quad \text{Baseline Utility}_{\text{Vacuum}} \le -\infty$$
- Equating ecological migration to vacuum enclosure is an ungrounded physical category error.

#### 3. Space-Based Solar Power (SBSP)
- **Consensus Narrative:** Beaming gigawatt-scale solar energy from orbital satellite arrays via microwave links provides clean, un-interrupted power for Earth.
- **Landauer & EROI Invalidation:** The launch CapEx, orbital station-keeping propellant costs, beam-attenuation losses across the atmosphere, and rectenna land-use footprint yield a drastically negative Energy Return on Investment (EROI) compared to local terrestrial edge generation:
  $$\text{EROI}_{\text{Terrestrial Edge (Solar/Geothermal/Nuclear)}} \gg \text{EROI}_{\text{Orbital Beaming}}$$
- Converting terrestrial energy and silicon into heavy orbital launch mass to generate continuous power violates Landauer heat dissipation bounds and net-energy scaling laws.

#### 4. Dyson-Swarm Post-Scarcity & Off-Planet Real Estate
- **Consensus Narrative:** Terrestrial resources are finite; moving industry off-planet to construct Dyson swarms unlocks infinite growth and post-scarcity abundance.
- **Coasean & Thermodynamic Barrier:** Exporting industrial manufacturing to orbital or lunar environments before achieving closed-loop terrestrial homeostasis ($\Delta \mathcal{S}_{\text{Biosphere}} \le 0$) exports thermodynamic instability across larger spatial scales at exponential energy transport costs ($\Delta v$).
- The capital required to build off-planet manufacturing yields vastly lower net physical utility than applying closed-loop Landauer-bounded material recycling directly at the local terrestrial edge.

   $$\text{EROI}(\text{Infrastructure}) > \text{EROI}(\text{Terrestrial Local Benchmark})$$
