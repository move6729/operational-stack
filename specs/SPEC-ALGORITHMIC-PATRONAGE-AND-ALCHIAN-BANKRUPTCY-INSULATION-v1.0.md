### 2.1 The Hirschman Captivity Rent ($\Delta R$) and Leverage Ratio ($\lambda$)

$$\Delta R_k = w_{\text{inside}} - w_{\text{merit},k}, \qquad \lambda_k = \frac{w_{\text{inside}}}{w_{\text{merit},k}}$$
