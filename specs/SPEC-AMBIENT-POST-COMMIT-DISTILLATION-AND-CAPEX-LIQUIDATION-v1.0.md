### 3.1 The Unidirectional Forward-Search Fallacy

$$N_{\text{forward}}(S_0 \to ?) = b^D$$

For a complex multi-step engineering or mathematical derivation with branching factor $b = 4$ and reasoning depth $D = 16$, an unconstrained forward search from $S_0$ without knowing the target patch $S_1^*$ requires exploring:

$$N_{\text{forward}} = 4^{16} = 4,294,967,296\text{ states}$$

This defense collapses once the verified post-commit terminal state $S_1^*$ (e.g., the exact Git commit diff + passing unit test suite) is publicly observable alongside the initial repository state $S_0$ (the parent commit + issue description). $$N_{\text{bridge}}(S_0 \leftrightarrow S_1^*) = 2 \cdot b^{D/2}$$

## 4. Pillar III — The Trade-Secret & Copyright Suicide Pill (`18 U.S.C. § 1839(3)`, `17 U.S.C. § 102(b)`): Why Claiming a "Distillation Lien" on Customer Output Triggers an Immediate Fortune 500 API Boycott

### 4.1 Statutory Extinguishment of Trade-Secret Status Upon Unconditioned Customer Output Delivery

Can a frontier AI lab prevent post-commit ambient distillation by asserting that its proprietary model weights constitute a trade secret under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 (`18 U.S.C. § 1836 et seq.`) and that model outputs carry a downstream "distillation lien" prohibiting third parties from training on customer-published code or documents?

Under `18 U.S.C. § 1839(3)(A)–(B)` (`DTSA-1839-3`), statutory trade-secret protection exists only if:

> "the term 'trade secret' means all forms and types of financial, business, scientific, technical, economic, or engineering information... if— (A) the owner thereof has taken reasonable measures to keep such information secret; and (B) the information derives independent economic value, actual or potential, from not being generally known to, and not being readily ascertainable through proper means by, another person who can obtain economic value from the disclosure or use of the information"

Furthermore, under Supreme Court precedent in *Ruckelshaus v. Monsanto Co.*, 467 U.S. 986, 1002 (1984), once an owner places information into the public stream of commerce or authorizes its unconditioned public release without confidentiality encumbrances, the trade-secret property right is **extinguished as a matter of law**. Similarly, under the Copyright Act of 1976, `17 U.S.C. § 102(b)` (`COPYRIGHT-102B`):

> "In no case does copyright protection for an original work of authorship extend to any idea, procedure, process, system, method of operation, concept, principle, or discovery, regardless of the form in which it is described, explained, illustrated, or embodied in such work."

And under `17 U.S.C. § 103(b)` and *Van Buren v. United States*, 593 U.S. 374 (2021) (`18 U.S.C. § 1030(e)(6)`), third parties who never enter into privity of contract with the AI vendor and who read publicly accessible Git commits, court filings, or package registries obtain those artifacts via **proper means** through open-gates public access.
